The mouse does the rest:

  • Drag a box on the chart to zoom in. Double-click the chart to return to the full view (Chapter 11: The Chart Info bar).
  • Double-click an item of the project, a stack or a layer to open its dialog (Chapter 2: Opening a demo project).
  • Right-click a stack to freeze or release it (Chapter 15: Freezing a parameter).
  • Press Enter after typing a limit or R min, so that the value is taken.
